Licensing

Dual-licensed: open core, commercial edition

MakhOS is dual-licensed — you choose the license that fits how you use it. The open kernel is free under the GNU AGPL-3.0; a commercial license covers proprietary and hosted use that needs to keep its changes private.

Open source

GNU AGPL-3.0

Free for everyone who shares their modifications — including when running a modified MakhOS as a network service. The AGPL's network clause (§13) means that if you run a modified MakhOS and let people interact with it over a network, you must offer them the complete corresponding source of your modified version.

Right for:

Research, teaching, hobby projects, open-source products, and anyone happy to publish their modifications.

Commercial

Commercial license

For products, appliances and hosted services that need to keep their changes private, or that want support and a warranty. Paid, per agreement.

Right for:

  • Embedding MakhOS in a proprietary device, appliance or product without releasing your source.
  • Running a modified MakhOS as a hosted / cloud / managed service with your changes kept private.
  • Organisations whose policy does not allow AGPL software.
  • Anyone who needs a warranty, indemnification, guaranteed support or custom work.
